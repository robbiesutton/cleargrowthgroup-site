Our Thesis

All value created for a customer or a business comes down to people deciding, step by step, to keep going: to sign up, to come back, to pay, to stay. Growth comes from understanding each of those decisions and making it easier.

Most companies look for growth outside the business: more spend, more leads, more new customers. But the largest untapped value usually sits inside the product and the customers they already have. Every product leaks, and most customers quietly stall before they get enough value to keep coming back and paying. Each point of friction makes every customer worth less.

The leaks are rarely where teams expect them, and guessing rarely fixes them. Finding them takes three disciplines. Understand how customers actually move through the product and where they drop off. Identify the few changes with the largest potential impact. Execute those changes as experiments, measure the result against a control, and only keep what works.

Because every change is tested, every gain is a number you can trust: revenue kept, revenue won, and value a buyer can underwrite. The largest companies work this way by default, with entire teams dedicated to it. Most companies have no one whose full-time job is this work.
