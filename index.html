Our Thesis

Every metric a company cares about is the end of a chain of human decisions. Revenue, retention, activation and referrals each add up the choices of many people deciding, step by step, whether to keep going. Growth comes from understanding that chain and making each step easier to take.

Most companies invest heavily at the top of the chain. They spend time and money creating demand and bringing people in, and they should. But every product leaks. People show up, some try it, and most quietly stall before they get enough value to come back and pay. Each point of friction makes every dollar and hour spent at the top worth less, and the loss compounds as spend grows.

The leaks are rarely where teams expect them, and guessing rarely fixes them. Finding them takes three disciplines, applied in order. Understand how people actually move through the product and where they drop off. Identify the few changes with the largest impact, and size them before building anything. Execute those changes as experiments, measure the result, and keep only what moves the number.

This applies to any metric at any company, because every metric rests on behavior that can be measured and changed. The changes that matter are often small. On one AI product with very low retention, mapping the funnel showed that users needed a second way in from the main app. That single entry point lifted retention by 20 points.

The largest consumer companies work this way by default, with entire teams dedicated to it. Most startups have no one whose full-time job is this work. Clear Growth Group brings that practice to the companies that need it, tied to outcomes we agree on up front and are accountable for moving.
