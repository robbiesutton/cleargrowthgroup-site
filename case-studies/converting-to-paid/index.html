Subject
Finding the customers who pay
Type
Case study · Monetization

Summary

The same AI product was built for a broad audience. One use case was worth six times the rest.

Results

6x
Revenue from users who booked appointments vs. users who didn't
3–4x
Aggregate increase in revenue per customer

The problem

The product targeted a broad set of users with many different use cases.

What I found

Users who used the product to book appointments converted to paid and generated 6x the revenue of users who didn't.

What we did

The company shifted its strategy to focus exclusively on appointment-booking businesses.

The result

Revenue per customer increased 3–4x in aggregate.
