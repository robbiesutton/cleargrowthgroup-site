Your value creation team

Summary

Large funds have teams whose only job is to move the numbers between close and exit. Most lower-middle-market companies don't. Clear Growth Group is that team.

The work is the same as in the case studies: find where customers leak, fix it, and prove it with experiments. Because every change is measured against a control, the gain is a number a buyer can underwrite, not a story.

1Three levers

  1. Retention

    Keep more of what you have

    Every point of churn removed is recurring revenue kept, with no new acquisition cost.

  2. Conversion

    Get paid by more of them

    Turn more trials, leads and free users into paying customers, and find the segments that pay the most.

  3. Activation

    Remove the friction

    Clear the steps that stop new customers from reaching value, so more of them stay and expand.
